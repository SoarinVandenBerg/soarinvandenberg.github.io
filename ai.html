My Thoughts on AI


There's a lot of controversy about AI coding (vibe coding), and since this is a website coded by me, lets quickly go through my stance on the subject.

I think that it depends on what you're doing and why. For example, my goal for this website is to not use AI at all. For some of my other projects like the ESP-32, AI did most of the code, because I couldn't achieve my goals without it. I think using AI is a choice that you can make, but you should make it clear that you used it. If your goal is to learn how to code, or you don't know how yet, then you shouldn't use it, but if you know how, and you're using it as a tool, that works.

What makes me want to be on the side of the vibe coders is just how much it gets hated on. Like nowadays if something cool gets released and it's revealed that they used AI to code/make it the project gets dismissed as AI slop. Sometimes the person's reputation gets demolished becuase of the use of AI too. People just take it way too far. For art, I totally get it! AI is making it so artists get backlash for doing anything, and more often than not art gets falsely flagged as AI.

Another thing is that people make hating AI their whole thing, and it really calls AI "haters" out of the woods when someone gets called out for using it. As an example, Hank Green recently had some backlash for using it for video scripts and coding projects. The scripts were bad, I agree. The problem is that sill today, if you go to one of his most recent videos and sort comments by oldest or newest maybe 1 in 10 is just someone hating on him. I have no way of proving this, but I think that these people are just coming to his videos to harass and not actual viewers.

Hack Club, a nonprofit that helps teenagers get good at coding and gives motivators for doing it, also has a history of disqualifying projects just for the reason that they used AI help. I've just started using the program, but from some quick research the only posts I can find on the company are complaining about how people's projects that they've spent 100's of hours on not getting approved. This is just SO STUPID becuase if theyre spending the time learning, WHY DOES IT MATTER IF THEY USED AI OR NOT. sorry for the rant.

I'm not even going to try to argue about the environmental impacts, I don't think I'm informed enough about the subject. I'm talking about using AI for coding here, but if you're using it for anything else, you should double- check the info it gives you.
